> [!caution] The Awoverse is eternally incomplete.

Welcome to the Awoverse. At least, its home on Obsidian and Quartz.
The Awoverse itself, if you're unaware, is the collection of universes created by internet user "awolen". It is called Awoverse because it is the uni**verse** of **awo**len's creations. These universes are found in many locations; Discord, Toyhouse... just about anywhere awolen has a social presence, Awoverse is quick to follow. The attempt of this website is to create one definitive source for Awoverse content, including the unpolished stuff that hasn't made it to Toyhouse yet. Easier than upkeeping a MediaWiki [trust me, i tried], and more organized than a shitty Discord forum.

The stories of the Awoverse are many with fluctuating amounts of elaboration. Many of these stories take place in a universe together, but many also belong to no world but their own. Isn't that lovely? Below, I will supply a short summary of every story here in alphabetical order.

[[Enzolore Index|Enzolore]] - a collection of characters linked by the bonds of family in a larger world.
[[FandomStoriesIndex|Fandom Stories]] - a collection of stories taking place in a fandom that awolen didn't create, such as fandom characters and universes.
[[HELLSEEKERIndex|HELLSEEKER]] - a story about the end of the world, in a biblical type of way.
[[isodromiaIndex|isodromia]] - a story about two minecraft players whose worlds seem to be one... somehow.
[[MiniStoriesIndex|Mini-Stories]] - a collection of stories that have no larger universe and usually contain just one character... all my one-shots and one-off ocs, basically.
[[OdditiesWithinOdditiesIndex|Oddities Within Oddities]] - a story about the gaps between universes in the awoverse, all barely connected. also called O.W.O.
[[PIndex|P]] - a story on hiatus about the incoming terror of god and the descent into a closet.
[[PaleMachineIndex|Pale Machine]] - a story about a young boy who isolates himself once he views the world as too boring and loses his mind.
[[REAPERVERSEIndex|REAPERVERSE]] - a story about the titular reaper and their desire to be human.
[[SAPIndex|S.A.P.]] - this one's complex... we can come back to this one later...
[[thewallIndex|the wall]] - a story about two men climbing a very large wall together.
[[UTFWBPIndex|U.T.F.W.P.]] - a worldbuilding project based on traditional fantasy tropes... this one really needs a name, i fear.
