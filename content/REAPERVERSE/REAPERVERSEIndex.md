>[!danger] REAPERVERSE deals with many darker subjects. these include religion, homophobia, child abuse, dehumanization, death, murder, and more. Take care of yourself!

REAPERVERSE is my most known project, I'd like to say. It's the closest to being finished [sad, i know], currently only waiting on the definitive version of one of the stories to be considered even a little bit finished in comparison to other things.

The main story of REAPERVERSE, currently, is split into three parts.
[[IterMortis|ITER MORTIS]], the main meat and bones of REAPERVERSE, following a pseudo-god and their journey to become human.
[[ActZer0|ACT ZER0]], a series of disconnected short stories that all happen prior to ITER MORTIS.
And an unnamed and undeveloped third story that documents everything after ITER MORTIS and the end of the world.
